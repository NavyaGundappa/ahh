​Our neurology department provides dedicated medical management for a wide variety of conditions affecting the central and peripheral nervous systems.

​Neurological Consultation and Diagnosis

​Treatment for Headaches and Migraines

​Comprehensive care plans designed to address chronic head pain. We help patients identify specific triggers, provide preventive medical therapies, and design acute management strategies to reduce the frequency and severity of migraine attacks

​Stroke Evaluation and Management

​Post-stroke medical management and preventive care to minimize the risk of recurring events. We focus on optimizing lifestyle factors, managing blood pressure and cholesterol, and coordinating with rehabilitation teams to help patients regain cognitive and motor functions.

​Epilepsy and Seizure Disorder Treatment

​Careful medical management for adults and young adults dealing with unprovoked seizures. Our specialists focus on finding the right balance of anti-epileptic medications to minimize seizure activity while prioritizing patient safety and daily quality of life.

​Parkinson’s Disease and Movement Disorders

​Long-term medical support for progressive neurodegenerative conditions. We design custom medication management plans to help control tremors, improve physical mobility, manage muscle rigidity, and sustain daily physical coordination.

​Neuropathy and Nerve Pain Treatment

​Targeted care for peripheral nerve damage caused by metabolic issues, trauma, or vitamin deficiencies. We address burning sensations, chronic numbness, and shooting pain through modern medical therapies and nerve-specific treatments.

​Treatment for Nerve and Muscle Disorders

​Medical evaluation and management for complex conditions affecting neuromuscular pathways. This includes diagnostic tracking and long-term care for issues like muscle dystrophies, myasthenia gravis, and chronic physical weakness.

​Multiple Sclerosis Diagnosis and Management

​Comprehensive medical therapies aimed at controlling autoimmune-related nerve inflammation. Treatment focuses on managing active flare-ups, slowing down disease progression, and utilizing supportive physical therapies to maintain long-term mobility.

​Memory Disorders and Cognitive Health Evaluation

​Detailed cognitive assessments for patients experiencing early signs of dementia or Alzheimer's disease. We offer guidance to families, prescribe supportive medications to manage cognitive decline, and focus on protecting lifestyle independence
