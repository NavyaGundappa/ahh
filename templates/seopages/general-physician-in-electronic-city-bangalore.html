What to Expect During Your First Visit

Your first consultation with a general physician usually includes a detailed medical evaluation and a clear treatment plan.

  • Medical History Review: The doctor asks about your symptoms, previous illnesses, current medications, allergies, lifestyle, and family health history.
  • Physical Examination: Your blood pressure, temperature, pulse, oxygen levels, and general physical condition may be checked based on your symptoms.
  • Diagnostic Tests if Required: Blood tests, urine tests, ECG, imaging, or other investigations may be advised depending on your condition.
  • Diagnosis and Treatment Plan: The doctor explains the likely cause of your symptoms and prescribes medicines, lifestyle changes, or follow-up care as required.
  • Specialist Referral if Needed: If your condition needs advanced evaluation, the general physician may refer you to a relevant specialist within the hospital.
