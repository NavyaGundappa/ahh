Privacy Policy

AHH-HRMS · Last updated: 15-10-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aarogya Hastha Hospitals Pvt Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the AHH-HRMS Android app (the "App"). The App is an employee attendance and leave management app intended for employees of Aarogya Hastha Hospitals.. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

  • Information we collect
  • How we use information
  • Data security
  • Data retention
  • Changes to this policy
  • Contact us

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Login credentials: your Employee ID and password, used only to sign you in.

Information collected automatically

  • Location, only at clock in and clock out: when you tap Clock In or Clock Out, the App records your device location at that moment, along with the date and time, to verify attendance. The App does not track your location at any other time and does not collect location in the background.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To record and verify your attendance when you clock in or out.
  • To process leave applications and approvals, and show your calendar and leave balance.
  • To display your profile information.
  • To operate, maintain, and fix issues in the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising.

3. Data security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption, secure servers, firewalls, and access control to protect your information. While we strive for full security, no digital transmission is 100% secure, and we welcome you to alert us of concerns at any time.

4. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. For material changes, we will notify you in the App or through your organisation.

6. Contact us

For any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us by email: itsupport@aarogyahastha.com, by visiting the page on our website aarogyahastha.com/app-privacy-policy, or by phone: +91 9036092727.
